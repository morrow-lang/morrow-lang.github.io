1//! Pure, deterministic supervisor engine following OTP 29.0.6 `supervisor.erl`.
2//!
3//! The engine is owner-local safe Rust: it owns no processes, reads no clock
4//! and performs no scheduling. A driver validates and starts a tree with
5//! [`Engine::start`], performs the returned [`Action`]s, and reports child
6//! acknowledgements, exits, deadlines, retries, clock readings, parent exits
7//! and management requests through [`Engine::handle`].
8//!
9//! Semantics: children start in declaration order, each waiting for its
10//! acknowledgement; a startup failure terminates started children in reverse
11//! order before reporting `StartFailed`. Permanent children restart on every
12//! exit, Transient children only on abnormal reasons, Temporary children never
13//! and their specs are removed. One-for-one restarts the exited child,
14//! one-for-all terminates every other running child in reverse order and
15//! starts all remaining specs forward, rest-for-one does the same for children
16//! declared after the exited one. Each automatic restart attempt is charged to
17//! a rolling whole-second window before the strategy runs; a failed start
18//! schedules a retry through the driver that is charged again and re-runs the
19//! strategy for the failed child. Exceeding the intensity, or a natural exit
20//! of a significant child under the configured auto-shutdown policy,
21//! terminates all children in reverse order and retires with `Shutdown`.
22//! Supervisor-induced exits are never restarted and never trigger auto
23//! shutdown. Graceful termination sends a shutdown signal and arms a deadline
24//! whose expiry kills the child; Infinity waits without escalation; Immediate
25//! kills at once.
