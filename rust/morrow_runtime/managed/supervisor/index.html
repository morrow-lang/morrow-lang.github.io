Module supervisor 

Source
Expand description

Pure, deterministic supervisor engine following OTP 29.0.6 supervisor.erl.

The engine is owner-local safe Rust: it owns no processes, reads no clock and performs no scheduling. A driver validates and starts a tree with Engine::start, performs the returned Actions, and reports child acknowledgements, exits, deadlines, retries, clock readings, parent exits and management requests through Engine::handle.

Semantics: children start in declaration order, each waiting for its acknowledgement; a startup failure terminates started children in reverse order before reporting StartFailed. Permanent children restart on every exit, Transient children only on abnormal reasons, Temporary children never and their specs are removed. One-for-one restarts the exited child, one-for-all terminates every other running child in reverse order and starts all remaining specs forward, rest-for-one does the same for children declared after the exited one. Each automatic restart attempt is charged to a rolling whole-second window before the strategy runs; a failed start schedules a retry through the driver that is charged again and re-runs the strategy for the failed child. Exceeding the intensity, or a natural exit of a significant child under the configured auto-shutdown policy, terminates all children in reverse order and retires with Shutdown. Supervisor-induced exits are never restarted and never trigger auto shutdown. Graceful termination sends a shutdown signal and arms a deadline whose expiry kills the child; Infinity waits without escalation; Immediate kills at once.
